Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Note: This is a starting template. Please review it, and take advice if needed, before relying on it.

Snovanta Systems Limited ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use personal data when you visit snovanta.com or contact us. We are the data controller for this information.

Who we are

Snovanta Systems Limited, registered in England & Wales, company number [COMPANY NUMBER], registered office [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS]. Contact: hello@snovanta.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you send through our contact form or by email: name, email address, company and your message.
  • Technical data such as IP address and browser type, processed by our hosting and security providers to deliver and protect the website.

How and why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss our services (legitimate interests, or steps prior to entering a contract).
  • To keep the website secure and working properly (legitimate interests).

We do not sell your data or use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run our website and email, including GitHub (website hosting), Cloudflare (domain and security), Zoho (email) and Web3Forms (contact form delivery). Some may process data outside the UK; where they do, appropriate safeguards are in place.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our providers may set strictly necessary cookies for security. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months unless it leads to a business relationship, in which case we keep it for as long as needed for that relationship and our legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email hello@snovanta.com.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
