Case study 01 · Banking · 8 months

Greenfield ServiceNow implementation for a UK bank

Greenfield Data migration Compliance Training & KT

The challenge

The bank relied on ageing legacy systems and a high volume of manual, email-driven work. Processes varied between teams, data was spread across multiple tools, and demonstrating control to auditors and regulators took significant effort.

What was delivered

  • Led requirements gathering and stakeholder engagement across business and IT teams
  • Designed and built compliant, auditable workflows aligned to regulatory expectations
  • Planned and executed data migration from legacy systems into ServiceNow
  • Ran hands-on workshops to educate users and build confidence in the new platform
  • Delivered process and operational knowledge transfer, backed by full documentation

Outcomes

  • A single, modern platform replacing fragmented legacy tooling
  • Manual hand-offs replaced with consistent, trackable workflows
  • Built-in audit trails supporting compliance and governance
  • Internal teams trained and able to run and evolve the platform themselves
Case study 02 · Telecommunications · 6 months

ITOM & CMDB transformation for a telecoms provider

ITOM CMDB Discovery Integrations

The challenge

The CMDB was incomplete and inaccurate, so teams didn't trust it. Incident, change and impact analysis relied on tribal knowledge and spreadsheets, slowing down resolution and increasing risk.

What was delivered

  • Worked with infrastructure, application and service teams to understand what data mattered
  • Identified the bottlenecks and data quality gaps behind the poor CMDB health
  • Defined and agreed CMDB health KPIs with stakeholders
  • Configured Discovery across on-premises and cloud environments
  • Integrated external systems so the CMDB was continually populated and reconciled

Outcomes

  • The CMDB established as the trusted single source of truth
  • Visibility across on-premises and cloud infrastructure
  • Agreed KPIs to measure and sustain data quality over time
  • Stronger foundation for incident, change and impact analysis
Case study 03 · Professional services · 4 months

AI service agent: Microsoft Copilot integrated with ServiceNow

Generative AI Copilot Microsoft Teams Knowledge

The challenge

Employees at a professional services firm spent time searching across SharePoint, Confluence and the service portal for answers, then raising tickets manually. The firm wanted a conversational, AI-first support experience inside the tools people already use.

What was delivered

  • Configured Microsoft Copilot integration with ServiceNow and built purpose-specific AI agents
  • Connected SharePoint and Confluence as knowledge sources to ground the agents' answers
  • Designed a clear exit route to a human agent whenever the AI could not help
  • Enabled the agent to create an incident or case automatically, pre-filled from the conversation so far
  • Integrated Microsoft translation services for multi-language support
  • Made the assistant available directly in Microsoft Teams for quick, in-flow help

Outcomes

  • Self-service answers available in Teams, where employees already work
  • Tickets raised with full conversation context, so there's no need to repeat information
  • Safe escalation to humans built in from day one
  • Support available across languages for a global workforce
